Solana Rent Reclaiming & ATA Hygiene

Solana reserves ~0.00203928 SOL (~$0.24 USD) per Associated Token Account (ATA) for rent-exemption storage. If a trader swaps 50 meme coins and sells out, ~0.10+ SOL remains trapped in empty storage accounts.

Automated Botsensai Architecture

When our engine liquidates a 100% position, the swap transaction bundle atomically appends an SPL CloseAccount instruction. This destroys the empty account and refunds the 0.00204 SOL back into liquid cash immediately.

.venv/bin/python3 scripts/reclaim_rent.py --close

Can Anyone Claim Other People's Rent?

No. The Solana consensus engine strictly verifies that any CloseAccount instruction carries the private key signature of the account owner. Third-party software claiming to "drain forgotten rent" from arbitrary wallets are malicious trojans.
